This policy explains what this website collects, why, who else sees it, and what
you can ask us to do about it. It was written from the site's own code, so it
describes what actually happens rather than what a template assumes.

## Who is responsible for your information

**Vision Accounting Pvt Ltd**, trading as tryACOwork, of Plot No.C, 001, Sector 16B,
Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301, India, is the controller of the information
described here.

For any question about this policy, or to make any of the requests set out
below, email **hello@tryacowork.com**. A person reads that address.

## What we collect when you fill in a form

There are two forms on this site, and between them they are the only way we
collect information about you directly.

**The free pilot request**, at /free-pilot. We ask for your name, your work
email address, your firm's name, your firm's website if you want to give it,
which US state you are in, which accounting software your clients use, a
description of the work you would like us to do, roughly how many hours a week
of bookkeeping you might hand over, anything else you want to tell us, and your
consent to us replying. Only the website field and the last note are optional.

The form also carries two hidden fields used only to filter automated
submissions: a field a person never sees, and the time the form was opened. They
tell us nothing about you and are discarded rather than stored.

**No form on this site asks for a phone number.**

## What we collect when you book a call

The calendar on /book-a-call is provided by **Cal.com**. When you book, you give
your name, work email, firm name and an estimate of hours **to Cal.com**, and we
receive the booking from them. Cal.com processes that information under its own
privacy policy as well as our agreement with it, and may set its own cookies
inside the calendar itself.

## What we collect automatically

**Analytics.** If, and only if, you press Accept on the cookie notice, we load
Google Analytics 4, which records your approximate location derived from your IP
address, your browser and device, the pages you look at, and the site or search
that sent you. We use it to see which pages lead to enquiries. If you press
Decline, or ignore the notice, **analytics is never loaded and no analytics
cookie is set.**

**Server logs.** Our hosting provider records ordinary request information -
your IP address, the page requested, your browser's user-agent string and a
timestamp - as every web server does. It is used to keep the site running and to
investigate abuse.

## Cookies and browser storage

Before you choose, this site sets **no cookies at all.**

Your choice itself is remembered in your browser's local storage, under the key
`tryacowork.consent`, rather than in a cookie, and it lasts twelve months. You
can change it at any time using **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page,
which clears the record and shows you the notice again. Clearing your browser
data also clears it.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets its own cookies to distinguish one visit
from another. If you book a call, Cal.com may set cookies inside the calendar.

One further cookie exists but is not for visitors: when a member of our own team
signs in to edit the website, a short-lived, strictly necessary cookie is set
for ten minutes to protect that sign-in. It is never set for you.

## Who else receives your information

We name them rather than describing them vaguely:

- **Vercel** - hosting. Serves every page and holds the server logs.
- **Resend** - email delivery. Sends your submission to us, and sends you the
  acknowledgement.
- **Cal.com** - the calendar on the booking page.
- **Google** - Analytics 4, and only after you accept cookies.
- **GitHub** - stores the website's own content and code. Your enquiry is not
  stored there; this matters only because our team signs in to it to edit pages.

Where we connect the forms to a spreadsheet or a customer-record tool so that an
enquiry is not held in email alone, that destination is added to this list before
it is switched on.

**We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.** We
do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

## Where your information is processed

We are based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, so our team reads and answers your enquiry from
India. The site is hosted on servers operated by our hosting provider, which may
be located in the United States, Europe or elsewhere.

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, that means
your information is transferred outside your own country. Where such a transfer
needs a legal safeguard, we rely on the standard contractual clauses in our
agreements with the providers named above. Ask us and we will tell you what is in
place for a particular provider.

## Why we are allowed to use it

- **To answer you.** You asked us to, by ticking the consent box. You can
  withdraw that at any time and we will stop.
- **To run and protect the website.** Our legitimate interest in a working,
  non-abused site. Server logs only.
- **Analytics.** Your consent, and nothing happens without it.

## How long we keep it

- **Enquiries and pilot requests:** twenty-four months from your last contact
  with us, then deleted. If you become a client, your records are kept under our
  service agreement instead of this policy.
- **Server logs:** as long as our hosting provider retains them, which is a
  matter of weeks.
- **Analytics:** according to the retention period set in our Google Analytics
  property.

## Your rights

Whoever and wherever you are, you can email hello@tryacowork.com and ask us to
tell you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. We will do it,
and we will not make you explain why.

**If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom**, you have the right to
access your information, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how
we use it, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent at any time. You also
have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

**If you are a California resident**, you have the right to know what personal
information we collect and who receives it, to have it deleted, to correct it,
and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. **We do not sell
personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural
advertising**, so there is nothing for you to opt out of - but you may still ask
us to confirm that.

We respond within thirty days, and sooner where the law requires it.

## Children

This site is sold to accounting firms and is not intended for anyone under
sixteen. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

## Security

Enquiries reach us by encrypted connection and are held in the accounts named
above, each protected by multi-factor authentication. The controls that apply to
your *clients'* data, once you are a client, are a different and much longer
subject, and they are set out on our [security page](/security).

## Changes

If we change this policy we will change the date at the top of this page. If a
change materially affects information you have already given us, we will email
you about it rather than rely on you noticing.
